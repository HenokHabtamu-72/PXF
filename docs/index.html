Synth

Two band-limited oscillators plus a sine sub, one low-pass filter, one ADSR. 8-voice poly, or MONO with legato note-sliding and a 0–2000 ms glide. 20 presets compiled in.

Grain

Drop in any one-shot and play it as streams of overlapping grains, pitched from the keyboard. Grain size 10–500 ms, density to 100/s, spray, ±24 st. C4 plays the sample at original pitch.
